Methodology & caveats

  • Base FTE only. Figures exclude employer superannuation, allowances, and loadings (including NT remote/location loadings, which are a substantial part of actual NT teacher pay).
  • "Top-of-scale classroom teacher" means the highest step a teacher can reach without promotion to Highly Accomplished Teacher, Lead Teacher, or any equivalent leadership/executive classification. The exact classification used per jurisdiction is noted in each detail card.
  • "Graduate" means step 1 of the entry-level classroom teacher classification.
  • Projections show only increases already agreed in each current Enterprise Agreement. When an EA expires, the projection line ends — subsequent pay depends on future bargaining outcomes.
  • EA status labels: current = in force and not expired; expired-in-negotiation = nominal expiry passed, agreement still operative while new one is negotiated; in-negotiation = new agreement being negotiated before expiry.
  • Every figure is sourced. Source links and access dates are shown in each jurisdiction's detail card.
